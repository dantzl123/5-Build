Workshops

Hands-on learning in editing, cinematography, and storytelling.

Our workshops give you time to practice the skills that go into making a film. You might shoot the same scene from two angles, edit the footage into a short sequence, or try changing the lighting to see how it affects the mood. Bring a project you’re working on or start with a simple idea and get feedback as you go.
